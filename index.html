Zipic for Mac

Zipic is a powerful, privacy-first image compression tool designed exclusively for macOS. It supports 12 formats including JPEG, PNG (including APNG), WebP, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, ICNS, SVG, PDF, and JPEG-XL, delivering up to 90%+ compression while preserving visually lossless quality. All processing stays local on your Mac—no cloud uploads required.
